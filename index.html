About Me

At 18 I joined the US Air Force because I wanted to serve, challenge myself, and do work that mattered. Over the next eight years, I led teams, managed healthcare operations and financial resources, and worked with people from different backgrounds around the world. Those experiences shaped how I approach my work. I learned to adapt quickly, take ownership, work through difficult problems, and make decisions when the answer is not always clear.

As my career progressed, I became more interested in the business side of the work. I wanted to understand why organizations operate the way they do, where they can improve, and how better information can lead to better decisions. That led me to pursue my MBA at the University of Florida. Throughout this rigorous program, I discovered that strategy and complex problem-solving were the areas I enjoyed most. I decided to continue building those skills with an M.S. in Business Analytics, to add a sronger analytical foundation to the way I approach business problems.

Today, I am able to bring my experiences together through work in business transformation. Throughout my career, I have found the most fulfillment in work that serves a greater purpose. I strive to continue giving back by sharing what I have learned, helping others grow, and contributing to the success of the organizations I work with. I want to do work that makes a difference.
