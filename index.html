02

About Me

I joined the U.S. Air Force at 18 because I wanted to serve, challenge myself, and do something that made a difference. Over the next eight years, I had the opportunity to lead teams, manage complex healthcare operations and financial resources, and work with people from different backgrounds around the world. Those experiences shaped both who I am and how I approach my work. I learned how to lead and work with different types of people, adapt quickly, take ownership, and find solutions when there is not always a clear answer.

As I progressed in my career, I realized I was especially drawn to the business side of the work. I want to understand why things work the way they do, find opportunities to improve them, and use information to make better decisions. That interest ultimately led me to pursue my MBA, where I discovered a real passion for strategy, problem-solving, and understanding what drives a business forward. I decided to build on that foundation with an M.S. in Business Analytics, giving me the analytical skills to approach those same problems from a new perspective.

Today, I want to bring those experiences together through strategy and consulting, using my leadership experience, business background, and analytical toolkit to solve complex problems and help organizations make better decisions.
