02

About Me

At 18 I joined the US Air Force because I wanted to serve, challenge myself, and do work that mattered. Over the next eight years, I led teams, managed healthcare operations and financial resources, and worked with people from different backgrounds around the world. Those experiences shaped how I approach my work. I learned to adapt quickly, take ownership, work through difficult problems, and make decisions when the answer is not always clear.

As my career progressed, I became more interested in the business side of the work. I wanted to understand why organizations operate the way they do, where they can improve, and how better information can lead to better decisions. That interest led me to pursue my MBA, where I found that strategy and problem-solving were the areas I enjoyed most. I decided to continue building those skills with an M.S. in Business Analytics, adding a stronger analytical foundation to the way I approach business problems.

Today, I want to bring those experiences together through opportunities in strategy and business transformation consulting. Using my military experience, business education, and analytical skills to solve difficult problems, improve how organizations operate, and support better decision-making.
